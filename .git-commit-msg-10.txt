Lecturers: add Subject, and auto-select the lecturer in the Printing Calculator

- Lecturer gains an optional Subject (subjectId -> Subject). The Add/Edit
  Lecturer form has a Subject box: pick an existing subject from the
  suggestions or type a new one. Typed names are matched case-insensitively
  (so "physics" and "Physics " are one subject) and a brand-new name is
  created in Settings > Subjects & Grades automatically; a deactivated match
  is reactivated. Clearing the box removes the link; a status-only update
  (activate/deactivate) leaves it untouched.
- Printing Calculator: choosing a Subject auto-selects the lecturer when
  exactly one lecturer teaches it. With several it hints to choose one, and
  with none it points to where to link one.
- Lecturers list shows a Subject column.
